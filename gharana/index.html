01 • OUR STORY

A small kitchen with a big appetite for tradition.

Simple food, carefully prepared.

GHARANA is a fictional Thakali restaurant concept created for this portfolio. The idea is a neighbourhood restaurant where the menu feels familiar, the portions feel generous and the room feels welcoming.

The menu keeps the heart of the meal simple: rice, dal, vegetables, meat, achar and the flavours that make you want another bite.
