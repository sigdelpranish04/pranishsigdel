01 • OUR STORY

More than just coffee.

Maya Coffee House is a concept café created around simple things: quality coffee, comforting food and a welcoming place to spend time.

Whether you're starting your morning, meeting a friend, studying for a few hours or simply looking for a quiet cup of coffee, Maya is designed to feel like a place you can return to.
