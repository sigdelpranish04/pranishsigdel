09 • FAQ

Before you visit.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome for selected services, but appointments are recommended for colour, treatments, bridal work and longer styling sessions.

Can you recommend a haircut?

Yes. A short consultation can cover your face shape, hair texture, maintenance routine and the style you want before the cut begins.

Do colour services require a consultation?

For major colour changes, highlights and corrective colour, a consultation is recommended so the stylist can assess your current hair and plan the service.

Are the prices final?

The prices shown are concept prices for this portfolio project. A real salon would confirm the final price based on hair length, product use and service complexity.
